Coursework · Artificial Intelligence and Applications

Brain tumour detection from MRI scans

A binary classifier that flags whether an MRI scan shows a tumour. I fine-tuned EfficientNetB3 from ImageNet weights on a small dataset of 223 scans and evaluated it with stratified 5-fold cross-validation.

Early runs leaned towards the majority class, which I fixed with class weighting. A learning rate of 1e-4 was unstable, so the final model trains at 1e-5.

Task
Binary detection: tumour or no tumour
Data
223 MRI scans, roughly a 2:1 class split
Model
EfficientNetB3 fine-tuned in Keras, with augmentation and class weights
Evaluation
Stratified 5-fold cross-validation
Result
85.0% mean accuracy, AUC 0.93
